# Aguinaldo Highway Corridor Spec (CommuTayo Pilot)

## Core Transit Nodes
1. **St. Dominic (Bacoor)**: Latitude 14.4503, Longitude 120.9492. Major UV and Traditional Jeepney terminus connecting Metro Manila (PITX) to Cavite.
2. **Imus Lumina / Robinsons Imus**: Latitude 14.4267, Longitude 120.9388. Key interchange point for mid-Cavite travelers[cite: 1, 2].
3. **Robinsons Place Dasmariñas**: Latitude 14.3312, Longitude 120.9575. Secondary boarding zone along Aguinaldo Highway[cite: 1, 2].
4. **SM City Dasmariñas**: Latitude 14.3218, Longitude 120.9634. Aguinaldo Hwy & Governor's Drive junction hub[cite: 1, 2].
5. **Pala-Pala Terminal**: Latitude 14.3167, Longitude 120.9650. Central transfer terminal for jeepneys heading to General Trias and buses heading to Silang/Tagaytay[cite: 1, 2].
6. **LPU Cavite (General Trias)**: Latitude 14.3056, Longitude 120.8992. Feeder terminus along Governor's Drive via Manggahan jeepneys and local tricycles[cite: 1, 2].
7. **Silang Premier**: Latitude 14.2341, Longitude 120.9744. Southern boundary corridor stop via modern jeeps and buses[cite: 1, 2].

## Transit Modes & Fares
- **Jeepney (Traditional/Modern)**: Base fare ₱13.00 (first 4 km), ₱1.80/km thereafter.
- **Bus**: Base fare ₱15.00 (first 5 km), ₱2.20/km thereafter.
- **UV Express**: Fixed point-to-point (Bacoor to Dasma: ₱50.00).
- **Tricycle**: Local terminal feeder (Pala-Pala to LPU gate: ₱30.00-₱50.00 special/shared).
