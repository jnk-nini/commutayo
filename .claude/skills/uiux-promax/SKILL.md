---
name: uiux-promax
description: High-polish UI layout, contrast, and typography rules for Philippine commuter apps.
---
- Palette: Dark base (Zinc-900), Light base (Zinc-50). Transit accents: Emerald-500 (Jeepneys), Amber-500 (Bus/UV), Sky-500 (Tricycles).
- Borders: Sub-pixel rings (`ring-1 ring-white/10`) with `backdrop-blur-md` on glass overlays[cite: 1].
- Typography: 11px uppercase tracking for signboard badges, 16px font-bold for landmark cues[cite: 1].
- Mobile touch targets must maintain a minimum height/width of 44px[cite: 1].
